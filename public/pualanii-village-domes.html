The idea

A retreat that takes care of itself, and of you.

Pualanii Village Domes is a self-sufficient eco-retreat and aeroponic community in Floyd, Virginia. It brings together geodesic dome architecture and modern aeroponic growing.

The domes make their own power from solar panels and wind turbines. Inside, each one has modern interiors and conveniences, so an off-grid stay still feels comfortable.

Aeroponic farming

Gardens that grow in the air

Aeroponics grows plants without soil. The roots hang in the air and are misted with water that carries nutrients. Because there is no soil or other growing medium, nothing has to be replaced or thrown away.

At Pualanii, aeroponic farming sits at the centre of the village, right next to the domes.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where is Pualanii Village Domes?

In Floyd, Virginia.

Is an off-grid dome comfortable?

Yes. Solar panels and wind turbines power the domes, and each dome has modern interiors and conveniences.

Where does the water come from?

The village uses an atmospheric water generator, which pulls moisture out of the air, and also collects rainwater.

What is a geodesic dome?

It's a round structure made from a network of triangles. Triangles spread loads across the whole frame, which makes the shape strong without needing internal supports.
